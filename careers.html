Content Coordinator

₦150,000 / month 95% remote · Lagos Flexible hours Reports to team lead

You own the content process, source what we need before we need it, help prepare content for production, and make sure it actually gets out.

See full role & apply Hide role details

What You'll Do

  • 01

    Manage our master content sheet

    We have recurring content formats and plenty of things we want to do. You'll help us keep track of them, source what we need ahead of time, and make sure our content is prepared well before it's due to go out.

  • 02

    Source content

    Find relevant videos, clips, and materials for our recurring formats, including Weekly Top 5 and Lineup Radar. This may involve following party stories while events are happening to find clips we can use.

  • 03

    Prepare content for production

    Find the right DJ photos, clips from YouTube sets, previously posted reels, titles, and other relevant information. Select the appropriate templates and put everything together into a clear framework our production team can work with. The goal is to get content ready up to the point of publication — not scramble to find materials when it's already due.

  • 04

    Contribute from time to time

    You should already be familiar with Nigerian electronic music events and attend them regularly. Occasionally, you'll also contribute short clips yourself when you're out. You're not our videographer, but being a roamer is part of the job.

  • 05

    Keep things moving

    You're responsible for making sure our planned content gets prepared and is ready to go out. That means following up, identifying what's missing, communicating clearly, and taking ownership rather than waiting to be reminded.

What You Won't Be Doing

You're not our head of content, and you're not our designer or full-time videographer. We already have a content direction, production process, and visual identity.

You won't be expected to come up with most of our content from scratch. However, we'd love you to learn the ropes quickly enough to understand what we're building and start contributing ideas and direct value.

We're a team of marketing people, so we like finding ways to automate repetitive work and avoid unnecessary busywork. We want to work smart, not just work more.

You'll report directly to the team lead.

What We're Looking For

  • Experience: At least one year of experience in content marketing or a related role.
  • Writing: Your writing has to be good. Titles, captions, briefs, messages — clean and clear.
  • Music: You're not new to the Nigerian electronic music space and its DJs, but you don't have to be an OG.
  • Interest: You're passionate about the music, the art, and the culture.
  • Organisation: You're comfortable managing spreadsheets, coordinating tasks, and maintaining timelines.
  • Independence: You can take initiative, work independently, and get things done without constant supervision.
  • Editing: You have basic video-editing skills in CapCut.
  • Phone: A good smartphone capable of recording quality 1080p, 2K, or 4K video is preferred. You don't need to be a professional videographer, but you should be able to contribute usable clips when you're out.

Working With Us

Flexible hours, but timelines matter. There's no rigid 9–5. You organise your own time, but we have things we need to get done, and we need someone who takes ownership, communicates clearly, and delivers without constant supervision.

This is a 95% remote role. The other 5% is when you're out at a party, at a brand engagement, or on a shoot with us.

Room to grow. There's a clear path from this role into a more strategic content position as the team scales.

Deal Breakers

We need someone who takes the role seriously, communicates clearly, and doesn't need constant reminders to get things done.

The first month is our getting-to-know-each-other period. If we realise we're not the right fit for each other, we'll have an honest conversation and part ways amicably. No hard feelings.

About AfroCue

AfroCue sits at the intersection of Nigerian DJs, the music they play, and the parties where it all comes together.

We believe there's so much more to tell about Nigerian dance music culture. From intimate club nights to raves, festivals, and even weddings, DJs are a constant thread connecting the music and the experiences around it.

AfroCue started in August 2025 with a simple idea: there were moments, sets, and stories worth documenting. Through AfroCue and Set Club, we're building the platform that tells them — and a team that runs it.

Nothing else open right now. If you're experienced in Nigerian nightlife, dance music, or media, we're always open to meeting good people — afrocueonline@gmail.com.
