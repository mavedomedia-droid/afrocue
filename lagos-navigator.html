Before you ask

The short answers.

Does it cost anything to answer the quiz?No. The nights we pick for you are free and yours either way.
Do I have to book anything?No. Answer the quiz and the nights we picked for you come by email, with the costs, and you decide from there.
Can I split a table with people I do not know?Yes. That is what the Section is for, and we tell you who is on it before you commit.
Why is the table priced from rather than at?Venue minimums move by the night. The 27th is not the 3rd. We quote the night you pick.
When do I hear back?The email lands within the minute. Check it for the full details and how to proceed next.
