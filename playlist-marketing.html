The Status Quo

Why
placement
marketing
is broken.

Most pay-for-placement playlist services share the same four problems. If you've spent money on Spotify promo before, you've probably hit at least two of these.

01

Pay fully upfront with no daily measurement.

Value is sold based on the playlist's total save count — not the streams your song actually earns. You write the cheque before you see anything happen.

02

Streams get inflated at the start, then taper.

Curators front-load activity in week one to make it feel like ROI. The rest of the campaign tells a different story — but the money is already gone.

03

Limited to existing playlist followers.

You're reaching whoever already has the playlist on their algorithm. No real-time optimization. No new audience discovery. No growth curve.

04

Spotify's own ads don't count toward revenue.

Native Spotify ads often reach free-tier users only, so generated streams may not translate to royalties. You're paying for streams that don't pay you back.

A Few Notes

How we
position
your song.

Position #2 or #3 — not #1

The #1 slot is the hook.

Position #1 usually goes to a well-known track that captures the playlist's category — the song that gets a cold listener to actually start playing. Your song sits at #2 or #3, where the engagement is highest. Same listener, same play-through. The familiar track does the door-opening for you.

Bonus placement — top 15

A second placement, often free.

We can place a second song from your catalogue further down the playlist. Roughly 10–20% of listeners who start a playlist make it to track 15 — meaning meaningful extra streams on a second track, with no extra spend.
