About AfroCue

AfroCue is creatively documenting culture — through spotlighting DJs, the parties, the players, and the music they play. Mostly dance music.

We’re also constantly looking at ways to impact the culture in creative ways — bringing experiences to you at every opportunity we can. Pre-Game with AfroCue is the first.

Pre-Game with AfroCue

A simple thing. We shortlist a few people living in the same area, pick you up, and give you a premium ride to the event — with a 10-minute photo stop at a public spot with a nice backdrop along the way.

And we intend to get you a sweet ride.

01

You and 2–3 others in your area get picked up in a premium ride. So if you’re on the mainland, your pickups stay on the mainland. Same for the island. Nobody’s crossing bridges to come get you.

05

Then straight to the venue. Dropped off in style. Your night continues at the party.
