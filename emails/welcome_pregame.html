About AfroQ

AfroQ is about creatively documenting culture.

We spotlight DJs, the parties they play at, and the music that moves people — mostly dance music.

We filter through the noise to show you what’s actually interesting. What’s actually happening. If you’re here, you’ll like it here.

What we’re building

PreGame with AfroQ is one of several initiatives we’re building.

We create experiences that extend what you see on our pages — turning moments into something you can step into. From curated rides into parties to the scenes around them, it’s all part of the same idea: making culture feel closer.
