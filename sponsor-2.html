Our First Initiative

Pregame
with AfroCue.

Pregame is the warm-up to the night, done properly. We shortlist a few people heading to the same party from the same side of town, send a premium ride to pick them up, and turn the journey into the first part of the experience — drinks in the car, a short photo stop at a nice backdrop, and content captured along the way before they're dropped at the door.

For a sponsor, the whole ride becomes your moment — the car, the drinks, the photos, the story. It's your brand woven into a night people were already excited for, not an ad they scroll past.
