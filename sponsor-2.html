Sponsorship

Two ways to show up.

Sponsor a party vs Own the moment

Both put you in the room. Owning a moment makes the whole experience yours — here's the difference.

Sponsorship

You share logo placement with the party's other sponsors — one of several names in the room.

You're our primary sponsor — the entire experience is built around your brand, not just tagged onto it.

Audience pool

Limited to the party's attendees and whoever caught it on their past flyers or posts.

All of that, plus our broader network of partygoers, DJs, tastemakers, media folks, and creatives who follow AfroCue.

The focus

The spotlight stays on the party — your brand rides along as a supporter.

The spotlight is on the experience your brand is curating for that party — unique, with multiple touchpoints and rich placement woven throughout.

Frequency

A truly great night doesn't come around every weekend, and you can't be sure the one with the most buzz is the one you backed.

You own a weekend on your terms — the moment happens regardless of what else is on, giving you predictable impact.

Access & inventory

Whatever placement the party includes in the deal — usually limited to logo spots and maybe a shoutout.

Free-access touchpoints for members, plus dedicated slots for media folks and DJs — a large media inventory pool to activate across channels.

About AfroCue

Where
Culture
Lives.

AfroCue documents and advances dance music culture — spotlighting the DJs, the parties they play, and the music moving the dance floor. From amapiano to afrohouse, 3step to afrobeats, we champion Nigerian nightlife with a culture-aware lens.

Our community are the tastemakers, media folks, creatives, DJs, and partygoers who set the tone for nightlife — mostly 22–35 and Lagos-based. When we reach them, your brand reaches the people others follow.

Our First Initiative

Pregame
with AfroCue.

Pregame turns the journey to the party into the first part of the night. We shortlist people heading to the same party, send a premium ride, and turn the trip into an experience — drinks in the car, a photo stop at a curated backdrop, and content captured before they're dropped at the door.

For a sponsor, the whole ride becomes your moment. Your brand owns the car, the drinks, the photos, the story — woven into a night people were already excited for.
