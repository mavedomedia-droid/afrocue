FAQ

Quick
Answers.

How long is the actual ride? +
Each hourly booking entitles you to up to 45 minutes of drive time from pickup. If you arrive at the venue before the 45 minutes is up, you can stay in the ride, wait outside the venue, or drive somewhere nearby — until the 45-minute mark.
How many pickups are included? +
Depends on your tier. Early Hours (3 PM – 10 PM) includes 1 pickup. Peak Hours (10 PM – 12 AM) and After Hours (12 AM – 3 AM) include up to 2 pickups — roads run freer at night so there's more room for extra stops. Extras must be on the route and within the 45-minute ride window.
Can I book for someone else? +
Yes — that's how a lot of our bookings work. Booking agents, event organizers and managers regularly book for the talent they handle. You'll fill in the talent's name and their email (the person actually being picked up), plus your own name and email as the booker. Confirmations go to both of you.
Is there a return trip option? +
Yes — it's optional and not charged upfront. Toggle it on at booking, give us a rough return time, and we'll confirm details closer to the day.
Do you cover the Mainland and Island? +
Yes — central Mainland (Ikeja, Yaba, Gbagada, Surulere, Anthony and thereabouts) and Island up to Ajah.
Is there a multi-talent discount for agents? +
If you're booking multiple talents for one event, WhatsApp us at 0816 237 3553 and we'll work out custom agent rates with you.
Can I change or cancel my booking? +
Changes — like updating your pickup location or shifting details — are fine. WhatsApp us at 0816 237 3553 as early as you can and we'll sort it. Same-day cancellations, however, are non-refundable.
