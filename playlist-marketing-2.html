The Status Quo

Why playlist
pitching is
broken.

Most pay-for-placement playlist services share the same problem: they sell you on total saves, not recent activity. And there's no way to know how recently those saves happened.

01

Save totals hide the real story.

A playlist can show 100K saves and still have almost zero people listening this month. The total is a screenshot, not an active audience.

02

Curators pump for two days, then stop.

You get a spike of activity in week one — just enough to make the numbers look plausible — and then nothing. The cheque is already written.

03

You have no visibility into who's actually moving.

There's no way to tell the difference between a playlist with 100K followers that grew 200 people last month and one that grew 8,000.

04

You're paying for someone else's dead audience.

And you have no idea until the campaign is over — at which point you've spent your budget and learned nothing.
