Who Is A
Roamer?
Roamers are people who live experiences in Lagos through parties and raves. In being present, your perspective is everything and as we document culture, we occasionally request your contribution in:
  • Simple & short surveys,
  • Tagging us in party and DJ moments you can find, and
  • Occasionally contributing on creative engagements that often come with benefits.

From time to time, we will notify you if any opportunities come up for contribution beyond just tagging us on your stories. These may include:

ACCESS

Go out as you would, we'll reach out when we have any extra slots.

FEATURES

Your video might feature in our content — we'll give credit as much as we can.

BRANDED MOMENTS

Initiatives like our Pregame series and Pregame Homies (coming soon). See example

CREATOR OPPORTUNITIES

Occasionally, paid or collaborative projects for creators in the network.
