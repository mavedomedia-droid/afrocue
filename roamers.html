Who Is A
Roamer?
Roamers are a small group of Lagos nightlife regulars selected each month.
Not because they have large followings. Simply because they are present.
They already go out. They already discover new DJs. They already experience the culture.
Roamers simply gives them a way to contribute to documenting it.
You Might Be A
Roamer If...
🪩

You attend at least two Lagos parties, nightlife events or raves most months.

🎛️

You know some of the local and international DJs shaping the scene.

📱

You occasionally capture moments on your phone, no matter how raw.

🔥

You can tell which DJ moved the floor the most.

💫

You genuinely enjoy experiencing the party itself.

Why Join?
AfroCue sits at the intersection of DJs, nightlife, dance music and culture.
Throughout the month, opportunities occasionally become available to Roamers.

🎟️ Event Invitations

Access to select events you already want to attend.

🔓 Event Access Opportunities

Occasional passes or list placements.

📡 Priority Information

Early heads‑up on upcoming events.

✨ Creative & Cultural Projects

Collaborate on content or campaigns.

📸 Features Across AfroCue Channels

Your perspective, amplified.

Slots, invitations and opportunities vary month to month and cannot be guaranteed.

What Do Roamers
Actually Do?
Nothing dramatically different. You continue doing what you already do.
Go out. Experience the music. Discover moments.
Occasionally tell us how it went. Share moments you captured on your phone.
The experience comes first. The best way to contribute is to actually be present.
Roamers are not observers standing outside the culture. They're people actively living it.
